™ — Structured Micro-Intervention Protocol

A structured micro-intervention designed to prevent cognitive degradation during pressure escalation. Applied immediately upon detection of micro-reactivity, mental noise escalation, or automatic rehearsal loops.

Detection Signals — When to Apply
SIGNAL 01
Micro-Reactivity
Shortened patience. Impulse to respond before processing is complete. Heightened sensitivity to external inputs.
SIGNAL 02
Mental Noise Escalation
Accelerated, fragmented thought. Difficulty isolating relevant signal. Internal volume increasing without resolution.
SIGNAL 03
Automatic Rehearsal
Preemptive internal dialogue. Simulating conversations or outcomes before they occur. Cognitive energy spent on fiction.
This tool is applied not after loss of control — but at the first signal of drift. The earlier the intervention, the lower the recovery cost. Precision requires anticipation, not reaction.

Resources — Application Guide

Application
Guide.

Practical framework for integrating NERVO™ into high-pressure executive environments.

01

Environment Setup

Apply the protocol in a private, controlled environment.
NERVO™ requires uninterrupted cognitive focus.

  • Silent or controlled-noise setting
  • All notifications disabled
  • Headphones recommended for acoustic precision
  • Seated, upright posture

This is structural application — not passive listening.

02

Timing & Cadence

Each day requires approximately 30 minutes of structured engagement.
Allow 24 to 48 hours between days to support cognitive integration.

  • Do not accelerate the sequence
  • Morning application recommended — before operational load begins
  • Avoid initiating sessions during active conflict or immediate crisis
  • Consistent timing enhances stability

Precision precedes progression.

03

Protocol Repetition

The Foundation Protocol is preventive.
Recommended maintenance cycle:

  • Full three-day cycle every 30 days
  • Full cycle before sustained high-pressure periods
  • Individual sessions may be applied selectively under pressure

Integration is cumulative.
Immediate transformation is not the objective.


Consistency preserves architecture.

04

What to Observe

During and after application, monitor:

  • Decision clarity
  • Reactivity thresholds
  • Cognitive load perception
  • Internal response calibration

Record observations privately.


Patterns become visible through repetition.
