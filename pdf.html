Resources — Application Guide

Application
Guide.

Practical framework for integrating NERVO™ into high-pressure executive environments.

01

Environment Setup

Apply the protocol in a private, focused environment. Eliminate auditory and visual distractions. Sessions require uninterrupted concentration.

  • Silent or controlled-noise environment
  • No notifications active
  • Headphones recommended for audio precision
  • Seated, upright posture preferred
02

Timing & Cadence

Each session requires approximately 30 minutes of active engagement. Allow 24 to 48 hours between sessions for cognitive integration.

  • Do not rush the sequence
  • Morning application recommended — before decision-making begins
  • Avoid sessions during acute stress peaks
  • Consistent timing across days supports integration
03

Protocol Repetition

The Foundation Protocol is designed for periodic application. Recommended maintenance cycle: once every 30 days, or before sustained high-pressure periods.

  • Repeat full three-day cycle as a preventive measure
  • Individual sessions may be applied in isolation during acute pressure
  • Consistency of application determines depth of integration
04

What to Observe

During and after each session, note internal shifts in decision clarity, reactivity thresholds, and cognitive load perception.

  • Use the session notes field to record observations
  • Track patterns across applications
  • Results are cumulative — not immediate
